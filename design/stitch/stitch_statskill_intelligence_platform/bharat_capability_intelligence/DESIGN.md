## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a premium, editorial-first identity for government capability building. It moves away from the sterile, utilitarian aesthetic of traditional civil service portals toward a vision of "Analytical Intelligence." 

The style merges **Modern Institutionalism** with **Editorial Minimalism**. It prioritizes clarity, intellectual rigor, and sophisticated data visualization. The aesthetic response should feel authoritative yet modern—evoking the feeling of a high-end intelligence briefing or a prestige publication.

**Key Principles:**
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast typography and intentional whitespace to signal importance.
- **Intellectual Depth:** Using serif displays and rich, muted earth tones to ground the digital experience in tradition and prestige.
- **Analytical Clarity:** Precise, thin-line data visualizations that avoid decorative clutter.
- **Asymmetric Balance:** Dynamic layouts that suggest progress and non-linear growth pathways.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in a warm, ivory primary surface that provides a "paper-like" editorial feel, contrasting sharply with Graphite text for maximum legibility.

- **Primary Surface (#FFF8F0):** Used for the main canvas to reduce eye strain and provide a sophisticated backdrop.
- **Primary Contrast (#1F2421 / #090e0b):** Reserved for core typography and primary navigation backgrounds to anchor the UI.
- **Functional Accents:** 
    - **Deep Teal:** Represents stability, health, and completed milestones.
    - **Saffron/Amber:** Indicates areas requiring attention or active development.
    - **Terracotta:** Used for high-priority interventions or critical gaps.
    - **Plum:** Reserved for "Intelligence" and "AI" layers—identifying insights generated by the platform's engine.
- **Soft Sand (#E1D9CB):** Used for subtle layering, panel backgrounds, and secondary borders.

## Typography

The typographic strategy utilizes a high-contrast pairing: **DM Serif Display** for editorial storytelling and **Manrope** for functional utility.

- **Display & Headings:** Use DM Serif Display for hero titles and major section headers. It conveys authority and a "printed" intellectual quality.
- **Functional UI:** Use Manrope for all interactive elements, metrics, and body text. Its geometric yet approachable structure ensures clarity in data-heavy views.
- **Labels:** Use uppercase Manrope with increased letter spacing for category labels and secondary metadata to create clear visual anchors.
- **Quotes & Insights:** Editorial "pull quotes" or AI insights should use the Serif face at a medium scale to distinguish human/system intelligence from raw data.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a **Fluid Editorial Grid** that allows for asymmetrical compositions and overlapping elements.

- **Grid Model:** 12-column grid for desktop with wide 64px outer margins to create an "airy" institutional feel.
- **Asymmetry:** Hero areas should utilize "off-center" alignments—for example, placing primary text in a 7-column span and allowing data visualizations to overlap or bleed into the remaining space.
- **Layering:** Panels and cards should use negative margins sparingly to "float" over the Primary Surface, creating a sense of depth without traditional heavy shadows.
- **Responsive Behavior:** On mobile, the 12-column layout collapses to a single column with 20px margins. Section gaps are reduced from 80px to 48px to maintain momentum.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through **Tonal Layering** and **Subtle Ambient Shadows** rather than stark borders or heavy skeuomorphism.

- **Surface Tiers:**
    - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Warm Ivory (#FFF8F0).
    - **Level 1 (Panels):** Soft Sand (#E1D9CB) or white with low opacity.
    - **Level 2 (Active Cards):** Pure White with a very soft, diffused shadow (15% opacity Graphite, 20px blur, 4px Y-offset).
- **Glassmorphism:** Use sparingly for navigation bars or AI insight overlays. A 12px backdrop blur with a semi-transparent Plum or Ivory tint creates a sophisticated "lens" effect.
- **Outlines:** Use 1px borders in Graphite at 10% opacity for secondary buttons or input fields to maintain a crisp, technical edge.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Rounded**, reflecting a modern, human-centric approach to government technology.

- **Core Elements:** Buttons, cards, and input fields use a 0.5rem (8px) radius.
- **Large Containers:** Section containers or featured panels use a 1.5rem (24px) radius to soften the layout.
- **Interactive Triggers:** Select small chips or status tags can be pill-shaped (full radius) to distinguish them from structural elements.
- **Data Visualization:** Nodes in competency maps should be perfect circles, while bars in charts should have slightly rounded caps (4px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Graphite (#1F2421) background with Ivory text. Bold, 16px Manrope.
- **Secondary/Accent:** Deep Teal or Saffron backgrounds for specific contextual actions (e.g., "Enroll" vs. "Review").
- **Ghost:** 1px Graphite border at 20% opacity.

### Intelligence Cards
- **Structure:** Use a 2px top-accent border colored by status (Teal, Saffron, or Plum).
- **Content:** Headline in Manrope Bold (Title-lg). Metadata (e.g., "Relevance: 92%") should use Label-md styling.
- **AI Insights:** A dedicated card style with a Soft Plum background and a thin Plum border to denote system-generated suggestions.

### Competency Maps & Data Viz
- **Style:** Use thin, 1.5px lines for connecting nodes. 
- **Nodes:** Glow effects (backlight) are permissible for active or "target" nodes using the functional accent colors. 
- **Typography:** Labels near data points should be small (Label-sm) to avoid cluttering the visual field.

### Input Fields
- **Style:** Minimalist. Bottom-border only or very light 1px surrounding border. 
- **Focus State:** Transition to a 2px Deep Teal bottom border.

### Status Indicators
- **Healthy/Target Met:** Deep Teal.
- **In-Progress/Attention:** Saffron.
- **Critical/Gap:** Terracotta.
- **AI-Enhanced:** Plum.
